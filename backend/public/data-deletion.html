FoodShare Data Deletion Instructions

How to request data deletion:

A user can request complete deletion of their FoodShare account and associated data (including WhatsApp linking data and messaging history) by contacting the FoodShare administrator through the contact email configured for the application.

1. What data is deleted?

Upon receiving a verified deletion request, we will remove:

  • Your personal user profile (Name, Email, password hashes)
  • Your WhatsApp identity mappings and phone numbers
  • Your active sessions and linking requests

2. Retained Data

For legal, auditing, and organizational integrity purposes, certain records may be retained or anonymized, including:

  • Records of completed surplus food donations (anonymized)
  • Aggregated analytics and forecasts that do not identify you personally
  • Financial impact ledgers bound to the overarching organization, rather than your individual user account

3. Processing Time

Data deletion requests are typically processed within 30 days of administrator verification.
