FoodShare Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to FoodShare. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share your information when you use our services, including our web platform and WhatsApp integration.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you provide to us when you register on our platform, link your WhatsApp account, or report/claim surplus food. This includes:

  • Contact information (Name, Email, Phone Number/WhatsApp ID)
  • Organization details and locations
  • Communication records via our WhatsApp assistant

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Facilitate the redistribution of surplus food
  • Send real-time alerts and notifications regarding available food
  • Verify the identity of users and organizations for safety and security

4. Information Sharing

We only share information with other verified FoodShare users (Kitchens and NGOs) to the extent necessary to facilitate a food rescue operation. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this policy, you may contact your FoodShare administrator.
